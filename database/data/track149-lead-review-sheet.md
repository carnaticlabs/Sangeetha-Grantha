# TRACK-149 lead review sheet

Owner: [TRACK-149](../../conductor/tracks/TRACK-149-tyagaraja-edition-consultation-collision-split.md). Every row is a **recommendation**; nothing here is approved. Only Seshadri can approve a claim, and an `approve` row stays `unreviewed` until accepted.

94 unreviewed leads with a value: 25 recommended for approval, 69 unresolved with a reason. Evidence comes from public Music Academy files and one open compilation (see [the sweep ledger](./track148-149-source-sweep-ledger.md)). Passages were located by OCR text search, so a miss can be an OCR failure. `unresolved` means no public passage was found, not that none exists.

The *Tiruvottiyur Pancharatnam* is named as a group by Sanskrit Documents (PDF p.2) but no public passage lists its members, so its member leads stay unresolved. *Deva sri tapastirtha*, the fifth Lalgudi kriti, and *Chutamu* and *Vinarada* (Srirangam) are named by the journals but are not among these leads.
