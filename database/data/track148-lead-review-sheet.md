## Findings beyond the 40 leads

- **Spec 9, *Samini Rammanave*:** the 1973 list (PDF p.42) names two tana varnams, *Samini* (Anandabhairavi, Ata, Kanchi Varadaraja) and *Dayanidhe* (Begada, Adi). The catalogue holds no varnam by Syama Sastri. *Samini* is therefore a distinct composition, not a mistitled SS-0036 (*nA manavini*, a Saurashtram kriti stored as Caturasra Ata). Adding either composition needs its own audited import decision.
- **Spec 6, Navaratnamalika:** two public sources agree on seven titled members (*Sarojadalanetri*, *Minalochani*, *Marivere gati*, *Devi ni pada*, *Devi Minanetri*, *Nannu brovu*, *Mayamma*). The 1954 article says nine and names the last two only by raga (Nilakurinji, Sri). No nine-member set is recorded. A candidate group claim exists for six of the seven; *Nannu brovu* (SS-0038) has none yet. Membership approval would trigger seeding, so it is left for a separate decision.
- **Spec 4, Todi swarajathi:** the 1973 list gives *Rave himagiri* (Todi) in Adi tala, and the Bhairavi and Yadukulakambhoji svarajatis in Misra Eka. The stored tala for the Yadukulakambhoji and Bhairavi pieces is Capu. Kalai and swara-sahitya counts were not found in any public passage.
- **Anandabhairavi raga-character claims (six disputed):** no new public passage was found. They stay disputed.
