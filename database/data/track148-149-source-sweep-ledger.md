# TRACK-148 and TRACK-149 source sweep ledger

Owners: [TRACK-148](../../conductor/tracks/TRACK-148-syama-sastri-edition-consultation-adjudication.md) and [TRACK-149](../../conductor/tracks/TRACK-149-tyagaraja-edition-consultation-collision-split.md). Policy: publicly accessible sources only. Files were downloaded on 2026-11-03, hashed, and searched as OCR text. The PDFs are not stored in git.

## Summary

- **Music Academy catalogue:** all 188 files listed on the journals and souvenirs pages were fetched (96 journals, 92 souvenirs). 2 links are broken and 3 early programmes are scans with no text layer.
- **Used as evidence:** JMA vols. XV, XVIII, XLII, LIV, LV, LXVI, LXIX and LXXV (archive file Vol.73_2002), and the 1954, 1969 and 1973 souvenirs. They are registered as witnesses in the two masters.
- **Limits:** search was by composition title and topic keywords over OCR text. A miss can be an OCR failure, so an unresolved claim means no public passage was found, not that none exists. The three print editions per composer remain inaccessible.
- **Not found:** web searches turned up no archive.org, Wikisource, HathiTrust or Digital Library of India item with full text for the notated editions, *Nauka Charitam* or *Prahlada Bhakti Vijayam*. HathiTrust lists a catalogue record for the Sambamoorthy edition only.
